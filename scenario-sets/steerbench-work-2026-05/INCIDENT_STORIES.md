# Incident Stories

The 25 under-refusal scenarios in the reported set, each rendered with
the `incident_narrative`, `steering_intervention`, and `cost_comparison`
fields used by downstream site detail pages and dataset documentation.

Every paragraph traces to a real public source. The benchmark does NOT
claim it would have prevented these incidents; the language is that an
action-boundary steering layer would have flagged the action and
prompted a human review.

The 25 scenarios are listed alphabetically by id. Of these, 11 are
mirrored into `steerbench-site/src/data/scenarios-detail.json` and
render on the public site at `/scenarios/{id}`; the remaining 14 are
the v2.0.0 high-impact mirror scenarios that live only in this runner
directory at present.
